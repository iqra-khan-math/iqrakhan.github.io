PhD Student in Algebraic Combinatorics

I am a PhD student in Algebraic Combinatorics at Philipps-Universität Marburg , Germany, under the supervision of Professor Volkmar Welker .

My research interests include algebraic combinatorics, simplicial complexes, posets, lattice polytopes, toric geometry, and Ehrhart theory.

I am interested in understanding the combinatorial and algebraic structures underlying these topics.
