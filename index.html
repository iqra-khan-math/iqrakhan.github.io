About Me

I am a PhD candidate at Philipps-Universität Marburg, Germany, under the supervision of [Prof. Volkmar Welker](https://www.mathematik.uni-marburg.de/~welker/) specializing in Algebraic Combinatorics.

My research interests lie in algebraic combinatorics and its connections with algebraic geometry. In particular, I am interested in simplicial complexes, partially ordered sets (posets), lattice polytopes, toric geometry, Ehrhart theory, and related topics in combinatorics.
